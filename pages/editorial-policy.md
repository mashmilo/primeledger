## Accuracy and sourcing
We base articles on primary sources such as regulators, government agencies, official provider documentation and peer-reviewed research. We do not publish unverified statistics.

## Independence
Advertising and affiliate relationships never determine what we write or recommend. Sponsored content, if any, is clearly labelled.

## Use of technology
We may use AI tools to assist with research and drafting. Every article is reviewed, fact-checked and edited by a person before it is published.

## Updates and corrections
We review articles regularly and show an updated date when changes are made. To report an error, email [akwbir@gmail.com](mailto:akwbir@gmail.com) and we will investigate promptly.
