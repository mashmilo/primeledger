## Who we are
PrimeLedger is an independent publication that explains insurance, investing, credit, taxes, business and technology in plain English for readers in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and other English-speaking markets.

## Our mission
Money and technology decisions are complicated. We turn complex topics into clear, well-researched guides so you can compare options and decide with confidence.

## How we work
- Every article is researched from primary sources such as government agencies, regulators and official provider documentation.
- Content is reviewed for accuracy and updated when rules, rates or products change.
- We explain our reasoning and clearly separate facts from opinion.
- We never accept payment to change the substance of an article.
