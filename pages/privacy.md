This policy explains how PrimeLedger ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website.

## Information we collect
We do not require registration. We may automatically collect non-personal data such as browser type, device, pages viewed and approximate location through analytics and advertising partners. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

## Cookies
Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and third parties use cookies to remember preferences, measure traffic and show ads.

## Google AdSense and advertising
We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalised advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info). Learn more at [How Google uses information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent choice for personalised ads.

## Analytics
We may use Google Analytics or similar tools to understand how readers use the site. Data is aggregated and not used to identify you personally.

## Third-party links
Our articles may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, or opt out of its sale or sharing. Contact akwbir@gmail.com.

## Children
This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy; changes take effect when posted here.
