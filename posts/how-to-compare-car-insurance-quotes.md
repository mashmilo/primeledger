Comparing car insurance quotes is the fastest way to lower your premium. Prices for identical coverage can differ by hundreds of dollars between insurers, so a few minutes of comparison pays off.

## Step 1: Know the coverage types
- **Liability:** pays for damage you cause to others; required almost everywhere.
- **Collision:** repairs your car after a crash.
- **Comprehensive:** theft, weather, fire and animal strikes.
- **Uninsured motorist:** protects you if the other driver has no cover.

## Step 2: Compare like for like
Use the same limits, deductible and add-ons on every quote, otherwise the prices are not comparable.

## Step 3: Check discounts
Ask about bundling, safe-driver, low-mileage, telematics and multi-car discounts.

## FAQ
### How many quotes should I compare?
At least three to five, including one direct insurer and one broker.
### Does comparing quotes hurt my credit?
No. Insurance quotes usually use a soft inquiry that does not affect your credit score.
### When is the best time to switch?
Shop 3-4 weeks before renewal or after a life change such as moving or buying a new car.
